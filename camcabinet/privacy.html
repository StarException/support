What we collect

We do not collect data through the app or operate an account or photo server. The app contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Photos and permissions

Camera permission enables the live viewfinder and taking photos. To import a photo, you choose it in the iOS photo picker. We request permission to add finished photos to Apple Photos. All photo processing, including adjusting the look of people in an image, takes place on your iPhone.

Local files, backup, and sharing

The app stores source images and finished copies so you can view and develop them again. It also keeps app preferences, camera recipes, and technical capture logs locally. The app does not sync your photo library. Copies saved to Apple Photos and iPhone device backups are handled according to your Apple settings. If you choose Share, the destination you choose receives the finished image.

Free photo allowance

We store allowance records in the iOS Keychain. If iCloud Keychain is enabled and available, successful-use records can sync to your other Apple devices through Apple. The records contain a camera identifier and a use identifier. They do not contain photos or payment details and we cannot access them.

In-app purchases

Apple’s App Store and StoreKit handle pricing, payments, subscription management, and restoring purchases. The app uses verified Apple transactions to check which features to unlock. We do not receive your payment details. See App Store & Privacy for Apple’s practices.

Deleting data and contacting us

Deleting a photo in the app removes the app’s copy. To delete a copy already saved to Apple Photos, use Apple Photos. Removing the app removes its local app storage. Keychain allowance records may persist across reinstallations. If you email us, we use the information you choose to send to respond to your request. For privacy questions or a request about a support message, use the address below.

svensuperman@outlook.com

隐私政策

数据收集

我们不通过 App 收集数据，也没有用于账号或存储照片的服务器。App 没有广告、分析或跟踪 SDK。

照片处理与权限

相机权限用于实时取景和拍照。导入时，App 只接收你在 iOS 照片选择器中选中的照片。写入成片需要「仅添加照片」权限。处理照片和针对画面中人物调整效果都在 iPhone 上完成。

本机文件、备份和分享

App 保存原片和成片，供你查看和重冲，也在本机保存设置、相机配方和拍摄技术日志。App 不提供照片同步。系统相册里的副本和 iPhone 整机备份由你的 Apple 设置管理。主动分享时，成片会交给你选择的分享目标。

免费出片额度

使用记录保存在 iOS 钥匙串。iCloud 钥匙串启用且可用时，成功出片的记录可能通过 Apple 同步到你的其他设备。记录只有相机标识和使用记录标识，不包含照片或支付资料，我们无法访问这些记录。

App 内购买

价格、支付、订阅和恢复购买由 Apple 的 App Store 和 StoreKit 处理。App 用 Apple 验证过的交易判断要解锁的权益。我们不会收到你的支付资料。Apple 的处理方式见 App Store 与隐私。

删除与联系我们

在 App 内删除照片会移除 App 内的副本。系统相册里已保存的副本请在 Apple「照片」中删除。卸载 App 会移除本机 App 存储，免费额度的钥匙串记录可能在重装后仍保留。如果你主动写信，我们会用你选择提供的信息来处理请求。隐私问题和与支持邮件有关的请求都可以写给下方地址。

App 內購買
