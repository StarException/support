隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 23 日

RemoStation 不收集你的任何个人信息。RemoStation 没有自己的服务器，也没有接入任何统计、广告或崩溃上报工具。游戏画面只在你的 iPhone 和你自己的主机之间、在你家的局域网里传输。

存在你设备上的信息

以下信息只保存在你的设备上，我们看不到，也不会上传给我们：

  • 主机的配对信息：主机名称、主机编号，以及配对时主机发给 RemoStation 的连接凭据。存放在系统钥匙串里，只用于连接你自己的主机。在主机详情页点「取消配对」即可删除。
  • PSN 账号 ID：配对时主机要求提供，你登录 PSN 后由 RemoStation 向索尼查询得到。只保存在本设备上，只在配对时发给你自己的主机。在「设置 › PSN 账号」里可以退出登录并删除。
  • 游玩记录：每次游玩的开始时间、主机名称、正在运行的游戏名称和时长，存放在本设备的钥匙串里。在「游玩记录」页面底部可以一键清空。
  • 免费试玩的用量：存放在钥匙串里。如果你打开了 iCloud 钥匙串，它会由 Apple 以端到端加密的方式同步到你同一 Apple 账户的其他设备，我们无法读取。
  • 设置：画质、手柄方案、显示选项等，删除 RemoStation 时一并删除。

RemoStation 会和谁通信

  • 你自己的主机：寻找主机、唤醒主机和串流的数据只在你的设备和你自己的主机之间、在你的局域网里传输，串流内容按主机的协议加密。
  • 索尼：登录 PSN 是在 RemoStation 里打开的索尼官方登录网页上完成的，账号密码直接交给索尼，RemoStation 接触不到。登录后，RemoStation 用登录结果向索尼的服务器查询你的 PSN 账号 ID；过程中产生的访问凭证只在内存中临时使用，不会保存。这部分受索尼隐私政策约束。
  • Apple：购买「完整版」由 App Store 处理，我们拿不到你的支付信息。为了判断网络是否可用（例如中国大陆 iPhone 的「无线数据」权限），RemoStation 会访问 Apple 的网络检测地址 captive.apple.com，不附带任何个人信息。

权限用途

  • 本地网络：在 Wi-Fi 里找到你的主机、把游戏画面传到设备上。
  • 无线数据（中国大陆 iPhone）：登录 PSN 和购买「完整版」。

儿童

我们不收集任何人的个人信息，也包括儿童的。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

RemoStation does not collect any of your personal information. It has no servers of its own and includes no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools. The game stream travels only between your iPhone and your own console on your home network.

Information stored on your device

The following stays on your device. We cannot see it and it is never sent to us:

  • Console pairing information: the console's name and ID, and the connection credentials the console gives RemoStation when you pair. Stored in the system Keychain and used only to connect to your own console. Remove it with "Unpair" on the console's page.
  • PSN account ID: required by the console for pairing. After you sign in to PSN, RemoStation looks it up from Sony. It is stored only on this device and sent only to your own console when pairing. Sign out and remove it under Settings › PSN Account.
  • Play history: start time, console name, the name of the game that was running, and duration of each session, stored in this device's Keychain. Clear it with the button at the bottom of the Play History page.
  • Free-trial usage: stored in the Keychain. If you use iCloud Keychain, Apple syncs it end-to-end encrypted to your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account; we cannot read it.
  • Settings: picture quality, controller layouts, display options and similar, removed when you delete RemoStation.

Who RemoStation communicates with

  • Your own console: finding, waking and streaming from your console happens only between your device and your console on your local network. The stream is encrypted as required by the console's protocol.
  • Sony: you sign in to PSN on Sony's official sign-in page, opened inside RemoStation. Your credentials go directly to Sony; RemoStation never sees them. RemoStation then uses the sign-in result to look up your PSN account ID from Sony's servers; the access token involved is used only in memory and never stored. Sony's privacy policy applies to this.
  • Apple: purchases of the Full Version are handled by the App Store; we never receive your payment information. To check whether the network is usable (for example the Wireless Data permission on iPhones sold in mainland China), RemoStation contacts Apple's connectivity check at captive.apple.com without sending any personal information.

Permissions

  • Local Network: to find your console on Wi-Fi and stream the game to your device.
  • Wireless Data (iPhones sold in mainland China): to sign in to PSN and to purchase the Full Version.

Children

We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
