有序相册

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 30 日

有序相册不收集你的任何个人信息。有序相册没有服务器、没有账号，也没有接入任何统计、广告或崩溃上报工具。整理、分类、识别都在你的 iPhone 上完成，你的照片不会上传。

存在你设备上的信息

以下信息只保存在你的 iPhone 上，我们看不到，也不会发给我们。删除有序相册会一并删除它们，系统相册里的照片不受影响：

  • 整理的结果：相似照片的分组、截图里读出来的字、照片的主题（人像、美食、风景这类）。截图里的字加了系统的文件保护。
  • 证件夹：只记下哪几张照片是证件、是哪种证件（身份证、银行卡这类），不保存证件上的号码和文字；照片本身还在系统相册里。这份记录加了系统的文件保护。
  • 私密空间：你挪进来的照片和视频存在有序相册自己的存储里，加了系统的数据保护，锁屏后读不出来；它们不跟 iCloud 照片同步。卸载之前，请先在私密空间里点「全部放回系统相册」。
  • 回忆影片的配乐：从「文件」里挑的音乐拷一份存在 App 里；从音乐资料库挑的歌只记下是哪一首。
  • 设置和你的选择：哪些照片点过「保留」、一行放几张、自己定的截图分类、年度回顾九宫格换过的照片、整库备份选的是哪个文件夹，都存在本机。

整库备份

整库备份只把照片拷到你自己选的文件夹（U 盘、移动硬盘、电脑的共享文件夹），备份记录也写在那个文件夹里。备份放在哪、谁能看到，由你选的那个位置决定；我们收不到任何副本。

有序相册会和谁通信

  • Apple 的 App Store：只在你点「连接 App Store」付款或恢复购买时。交易由 Apple 处理，我们拿不到你的支付信息。
  • 苹果地图：看地图时（回忆里的地图、照片信息里的小地图），从苹果地图加载底图；在中国大陆，地图数据由高德提供。苹果能知道你在看哪一片地图，但照片本身、照片里记的位置都不会发给任何人。
  • iCloud 照片：你的照片存在 iCloud 照片里、iPhone 上只有缩略图时（开了「优化 iPhone 储存空间」），看照片和原图、导出视频、分享前打码、整库备份、挪进私密空间这些操作，会由系统的照片功能从你自己的 iCloud 下载。这是 Apple 的服务，有序相册只是请系统取你自己的照片，不经过我们，也不上传任何东西。
  • Apple Music：给回忆影片挑的歌如果还没下载到音乐资料库，由系统的音乐播放器在线播放。

除此之外，有序相册不联网。照片拍在哪（「杭州 西湖区」这种）、足迹点亮的省份、旅程和回忆，都是用 App 里自带的地名表在本机算的。付费页上的「使用条款」会在 Safari 里打开 Apple 的标准许可协议。

分享出去的图片

「分享前检查」能给手机号、地址、订单号、二维码打码，并去掉照片里记的位置和机型。打码用的是马赛克或模糊，存出来的图片里已经没有原来的内容，还原不回去。

权限用途

  • 照片：整理、分类、找相似、回忆和备份都要读取相册，只在本机处理；你也可以只允许部分照片。删除照片时，每次都由系统弹窗让你确认，删掉的先进「最近删除」，30 天内能找回。
  • 照片（添加）：把打过码、加了水印的图片，做好的海报，还有从私密空间放回去的照片存进相册。
  • 面容 ID：只用来打开证件夹和私密空间，由系统验证，我们拿不到你的面容数据。
  • 媒体与 Apple Music：只在你给回忆影片挑资料库里的歌时才会问；歌由系统的音乐播放器播放，还没下载的会在线播放。
  • 无线数据（中国大陆的 iPhone）：付款、恢复购买，以及加载地图底图。

有序相册不申请定位、相机、麦克风、通讯录和通知权限。以上权限都可以拒绝；除了照片权限，拒绝其中任何一项都不影响整理和清理。

儿童

我们不收集任何人的个人信息，也包括儿童的。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

Youxu (有序相册) does not collect any of your personal information. It has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools. Organizing, sorting and recognition all happen on your iPhone, and your photos are never uploaded.

Information stored on your device

The following stays on your iPhone. We cannot see it and it is never sent to us. Deleting Youxu deletes it; the photos in your photo library are not affected:

  • Organizing results: groups of similar photos, the text read from screenshots, and photo themes (portraits, food, scenery and the like). The screenshot text is stored with iOS file protection.
  • ID Vault: only which photos are ID documents and what kind (ID card, bank card and the like). Document numbers and text are not stored, and the photos themselves stay in your photo library. This record is stored with iOS file protection.
  • Private Space: photos and videos you move in are stored inside Youxu with iOS data protection, unreadable while the phone is locked, and they don’t sync with iCloud Photos. Before uninstalling, tap “Restore all to Photos” in Private Space.
  • Memory movie music: music you pick from Files is copied into the app; for a song from your music library, only which song it is is remembered.
  • Settings and choices: which photos you marked to keep, how many photos per row, your own screenshot categories, photos you swapped in a Year in Review grid, and which folder Full Backup uses are all stored on the device.

Full Backup

Full Backup copies photos only to a folder you choose (a USB drive, an external disk, a shared folder on your computer), and its backup record is written into that same folder. Where the backup lives and who can see it depends on the location you pick; we never receive a copy.

Who Youxu communicates with

  • Apple’s App Store: only when you tap “Connect to the App Store” to buy or restore a purchase. Transactions are handled by Apple; we never receive your payment information.
  • Apple Maps: when you look at a map (the map in Memories, the small map in a photo’s info), map tiles load from Apple Maps; in mainland China the map data is provided by AutoNavi (Amap). Apple can know which area of the map you are viewing, but your photos and the locations recorded in them are never sent to anyone.
  • iCloud Photos: when your photos are stored in iCloud Photos and only thumbnails are on your iPhone (with “Optimize iPhone Storage” on), viewing photos and originals, exporting videos, masking before sharing, Full Backup and moving photos into Private Space make iOS download them from your own iCloud. This is Apple’s service; Youxu only asks iOS for your own photos, nothing goes through us and nothing is uploaded.
  • Apple Music: if a song you picked for a memory movie has not been downloaded to your music library, the system music player streams it.

Apart from this, Youxu does not go online. Where a photo was taken (“Xihu District, Hangzhou”), the provinces lit up in Footprints, trips and memories are all worked out on the device from a place-name table built into the app. The “Terms of Use” link on the purchase page opens Apple’s standard license agreement in Safari.

Images you share

Check Before Sharing can mask phone numbers, addresses, order numbers and QR codes, and removes the location and device model recorded in the photo. Masking uses mosaic or blur, so the saved image no longer contains the original content and cannot be restored.

Permissions

  • Photos: organizing, sorting, finding similar photos, memories and backup all read your photo library, and everything is processed on the device; you can also allow access to selected photos only. Every deletion asks for your confirmation in a system dialog, and deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days.
  • Photos (add only): to save masked or watermarked images, the posters you make, and photos restored from Private Space into your library.
  • Face ID: only to open ID Vault and Private Space. iOS does the check; we never receive your face data.
  • Media & Apple Music: asked only when you pick a song from your library for a memory movie; the song is played by the system music player, and songs not yet downloaded are streamed.
  • Wireless Data (iPhones sold in mainland China): for purchases, restoring purchases and loading map tiles.

Youxu does not ask for location, camera, microphone, contacts or notification permissions. You can decline any permission; apart from Photos, declining any of them does not affect organizing and cleaning up.

Children

We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
