记着呢

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 4 日

记着呢不收集你的任何个人信息。记着呢没有服务器、没有账号，也没有接入任何统计、广告或崩溃上报工具。你写的备忘存在你的 iPhone 上；打开 iCloud 备份以后，备份到你自己的 iCloud。

存在你设备上的信息

以下信息只保存在你的 iPhone 上，我们看不到，也不会发给我们。删除记着呢会一并删除它们：

  • 备忘：正文、图片、提醒、历史版本、文件夹和标签。删掉的先进回收站，放满 90 天才真删。
  • 每日快照：每天给全部备忘拍一张，留最近 30 张，删错、改乱时从这里找回。
  • 私密文件夹：里面的备忘和图用苹果系统自带的加密（CryptoKit）封好了存，这台手机上、每日快照里、iCloud 上都是加密的，打开要面容 ID 或锁屏密码。钥匙存在这台手机的钥匙串里；开着 iCloud 钥匙串，会跟到你的其他 Apple 设备。恢复码只在你手里，我们没有备份：钥匙和恢复码都丢了，谁也打不开。
  • 给小组件看的摘要：今天要记得的事、置顶的备忘，放在 App 和小组件共用的地方，只给这台手机上的小组件、锁屏、Siri 看。私密文件夹里的备忘不在里面，上面的提醒只写「私密提醒」。
  • 设置和你的选择：排序方式、关过哪些提示、iCloud 备份开没开、买没买过 Pro，都存在本机。

iPhone 的整机备份（iCloud 云备份、备份到电脑）会带上这些信息；私密文件夹里的在备份里也是加密的。

iCloud 备份

默认关着，你在「数据安全」里打开才备份。打开以后，备忘备份到你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库，只有登录同一个 Apple 账户的设备读得到；在中国大陆，iCloud 由云上贵州运营。正文、提醒、文件夹名、图里认出的字加密了再存进去；我们没有办法看你 iCloud 里的东西。关掉备份，这台手机上的备忘一条都不会少。

记着呢会和谁通信

  • Apple 的 iCloud：只在你打开了 iCloud 备份时，备份到你自己的 iCloud。
  • Apple 的 App Store：你在 Pro 页点「购买 Pro」「恢复购买」或「用兑换码」时；点过以后，每次打开还会核对一遍是否买过（家人共享、退款要能收到）。交易由 Apple 处理，我们拿不到你的支付信息。

除此之外，记着呢不联网。「关于」和付费页上的「使用条款」「技术支持」会在 Safari 里打开 Apple 的标准许可协议和本网站。

说话、拍照、剪贴板和短信

  • 说话转文字（首页点一下说话、编辑时说话）：你说的话在这台手机上转成文字，不上传。只用手机上的识别：iOS 26 起用系统新的语音识别，这台手机上还没有它的语音模型时，系统会从苹果下载一份（系统自带的 App 也用这一份），只是下载，你说的话照样在本机处理；iOS 26 以前用听写，还没下载中文听写时，会请你先到系统设置里打开，不会交给别处去转。系统的语音识别授权框里有一段「语音数据将发送给 Apple」的统一说明，记着呢用的是手机上的识别，录音不会发出这台手机。
  • 原声（1.3 起）：在首页说话记下的备忘留着原声，点开那条就能听。原声只存在这台手机上，不上传，记着呢的 iCloud 备份里也没有它；私密备忘的原声和正文一样封好了存。可以在那条备忘里删掉原声；备忘彻底删除后，原声再留 35 天（和图片一样，从每日快照找回备忘时原声还在）就删掉。在备忘里点麦克风说的话只转成文字，不留原声；导入的录音转完就删。
  • 录一句当铃声（1.2 起）：给强提醒录的那一句存在这台手机上，只拿来当铃声，不上传，记着呢的 iCloud 备份里也没有它；删了那条提醒就一起删掉。
  • 拍照认字：照片、截图里的字用 iPhone 自带的文字识别在本机认出来。
  • 剪贴板：回到首页时只看剪贴板里有没有新复制的字，不读内容；你点「粘贴」才读。
  • 取件短信（1.2 起）：记着呢读不到你的短信。只有你在「快捷指令」里设了「收取件码」自动化，短信来了，快捷指令才把那一条交给记着呢，在这台手机上认：认出取件码的存成一条备忘，认不出的不留。

分享、导出和搬家

分享为长图、分享文字、导出全部、从苹果备忘录搬过来，走的都是系统的分享面板和「文件」App，东西去哪儿由你选。导出的 Markdown 文件不加密，留着原声的备忘旁边放一个同名的 .m4a；连私密备忘一起导出前，要先用面容 ID 确认。系统分享面板里的「记着呢」只把你分享的内容交给记着呢，不联网。

权限用途

  • 相机：拍照片、扫描白板和票据存进备忘，在本机认出上面的字。
  • 照片（添加）：把备忘里的图片、排好的长图存进相册。只往相册里放，不读相册里别的照片。
  • 麦克风和语音识别：点一下说话、编辑时说话，在本机转成文字，首页说话记下的留着原声；录一句当强提醒的铃声。
  • 通知：到点提醒你。「重要」提醒是时间敏感通知，开着专注模式也会弹出来。
  • 闹钟（iOS 26）：只在你把提醒设成「强提醒」时才问，到点像闹钟一样整屏响。
  • 面容 ID：只用来打开私密文件夹，由系统验证，我们拿不到你的面容数据。
  • 无线数据（中国大陆的 iPhone）：iCloud 备份，以及连接 App Store。

记着呢不申请定位、通讯录和读取相册的权限。以上权限都是第一次用到时才问，都可以拒绝，拒绝其中任何一项都不影响写备忘。

儿童

我们不收集任何人的个人信息，也包括儿童的。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Memo Keeper (记着呢) does not collect any of your personal information. It has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools. Your notes stay on your iPhone; if you turn on iCloud Backup in Memo Keeper, they are backed up to your own iCloud.

Information stored on your device

The following stays on your iPhone. We cannot see it and it is never sent to us. Deleting Memo Keeper deletes it:

  • Notes: text, images, reminders, version history, folders and tags. Deleted notes go to the Trash and are removed for good after 90 days.
  • Daily snapshots: one copy of all notes per day, the latest 30 kept, for recovering from mistakes.
  • Private folders: notes and images inside are encrypted with Apple’s built-in encryption (CryptoKit) on the device, in daily snapshots and in iCloud, and open with Face ID or the device passcode. The key is kept in the device’s keychain and, with iCloud Keychain on, follows you to your other Apple devices. Only you hold the recovery code; we keep no copy, so if both the key and the recovery code are lost, nobody can open these notes.
  • Widget summary: today’s reminders and pinned notes, kept in storage shared between the app and its widgets, read only by widgets, the Lock Screen and Siri on this iPhone. Notes in private folders are not included, and their reminders show only “Private reminder”.
  • Settings and choices: sort order, dismissed tips, whether iCloud Backup is on, and whether you bought Pro are stored on the device.

Your iPhone’s own backups (the iPhone’s iCloud Backup, or backups to a computer) include this information; notes in private folders stay encrypted in backups.

iCloud Backup in Memo Keeper

Off by default; it backs up only after you turn it on in Data Safety. Notes are then backed up to your own private iCloud database, readable only by devices signed in to the same Apple Account; in mainland China, iCloud is operated by GCBD (Guizhou-Cloud Big Data). Note text, reminders, folder names and text recognized in images are encrypted before they are stored there, and we have no way to see what is in your iCloud. Turning the backup off never removes a note from this iPhone.

Who Memo Keeper communicates with

  • Apple iCloud: only while iCloud Backup is on in Memo Keeper, to your own iCloud.
  • Apple’s App Store: when you tap “Get Pro”, “Restore Purchases” or “Redeem Code” on the Pro page; after that, a check at each launch so Family Sharing and refunds come through. Transactions are handled by Apple; we never receive your payment information.

Apart from this, Memo Keeper does not go online. The “Terms of Use” and “Support” links open Apple’s standard license agreement and this website in Safari.

Voice, photos, the clipboard and texts

  • Speech to text (Tap to Talk and dictating while editing): speech is transcribed on the device and is never uploaded. Memo Keeper uses on-device recognition only. From iOS 26 it uses Apple’s new speech recognition; if its speech model is not on the iPhone yet, iOS downloads it from Apple (the same model the built-in apps use), and your speech is still processed on the device. On earlier iOS versions it uses dictation, and if Chinese dictation has not been downloaded yet, it asks you to turn it on in Settings rather than sending audio elsewhere. The system’s speech-recognition permission prompt includes Apple’s general note that speech data is sent to Apple; with on-device recognition, the recording never leaves the iPhone.
  • Original audio (from 1.3): notes you make by talking on the main screen keep the original audio, so you can open the note and play it back. The audio is stored only on this iPhone, is never uploaded and is not part of Memo Keeper’s iCloud Backup; in private notes it is encrypted like the text. You can delete it from the note. After a note is permanently deleted, its audio is kept for 35 more days (like images, so a note recovered from a daily snapshot still has it) and then deleted. Dictating inside a note only turns speech into text and keeps no audio; imported recordings are deleted once transcribed.
  • Recorded alarm sound (from 1.2): a line you record for an alarm reminder is kept on this iPhone and used only as its alarm sound. It is never uploaded, is not part of Memo Keeper’s iCloud Backup, and is deleted along with the reminder.
  • Text in photos: text in photos and screenshots is recognized on the device with iOS’s built-in text recognition.
  • Clipboard: when you return to the home screen, Memo Keeper only checks whether new text was copied, without reading it; it reads the clipboard only when you tap Paste.
  • Pickup texts (from 1.2): Memo Keeper cannot read your messages. Only if you set up the “Save Pickup Code” automation in Shortcuts does Shortcuts hand a text to Memo Keeper when it arrives. Memo Keeper reads it on the device and saves it as a note if it finds a pickup code; otherwise nothing is kept.

Sharing, export and import

Share as image, share text, Export All and importing from Apple Notes all use the system share sheet and the Files app, so you decide where things go. Exported Markdown files are not encrypted, and a note that keeps its original audio gets an .m4a file with the same name next to it; exporting private notes requires Face ID first. The Memo Keeper share extension only hands what you share to Memo Keeper and does not go online.

Permissions

  • Camera: to take photos and scan whiteboards and receipts into notes, reading the text on the device.
  • Photos (add only): to save images from notes and long images to your library. Memo Keeper never reads other photos.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition: for Tap to Talk and dictating while editing, transcribed on the device (notes made on the main screen also keep the original audio on the device), and for recording a line as an alarm sound.
  • Notifications: to remind you on time. “Important” reminders are Time Sensitive and appear even with a Focus on.
  • Alarms (iOS 26): asked only when you make a reminder a “Strong reminder”, which rings like an alarm.
  • Face ID: only to open private folders. iOS does the check; we never receive your face data.
  • Wireless Data (iPhones sold in mainland China): for iCloud Backup and the App Store.

Memo Keeper does not ask for location, contacts or photo library read access. Every permission is requested the first time it is needed, and declining any of them does not stop you from writing notes.
