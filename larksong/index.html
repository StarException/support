Larksong: Text to Speech, for iPhone

Larksong reads your documents, web pages and scanned pages aloud with natural voices that run on your iPhone. No account, works offline, and nothing you add is uploaded.

Contact

Something not working, or a document that reads badly? Write to us, or tap Settings › Contact Support in Larksong:

svensuperman@outlook.com

It helps to attach diagnostics: in Larksong, open Settings › Send Diagnostics and choose Mail. The diagnostics don't include the text or titles of your documents.

Questions

What can I add?

PDFs, EPUBs, Word documents (.docx), plain text, Markdown, RTF and HTML files; web links; pages shared from Safari; photos of printed pages (Scan); and text you paste. Tap + in the library. You can also share a page from Safari: tap Share, then Larksong.

A web page won't import.

Some pages need you to sign in, or build their text with scripts. Open the page in Safari, sign in if needed, then tap Share › Larksong; Larksong takes the page you see. A few sites hide their text from other apps on purpose, and Larksong can't read those.

What's the difference between the voices?

  • Natural: the most lifelike voices, made on your iPhone. They come with the app.
  • Lite: lighter natural voices that use less battery. Your iPhone downloads them from Apple after you install Larksong, so they may appear a little later.
  • Apple: the voices built into iOS. Free with no limits. For better ones, open the Settings app, then Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices, and download an Enhanced or Premium voice.

Natural and Lite voices read English. Documents in other languages are read with an Apple voice in that language.

What's free, and what's Pro?

Apple voices and every feature for adding and reading documents are free. Natural and Lite voices come with 3 hours of free listening (only time they are actually speaking counts). After that, Larksong finishes the paragraph and continues with an Apple voice. Natural Voices Pro removes the limit and lets you prepare whole books ahead of time. It's available as a one-time purchase or a yearly subscription, and you can share it with your family through Family Sharing.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Open Settings in Larksong and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I cancel the yearly subscription?

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions › Larksong. Audio you already prepared stays on your iPhone.

Why did the voice change while I was listening?

If speech can't be made fast enough to keep playing, for example when your iPhone is locked and charging (iOS slows apps down then) or getting hot, Larksong switches to a lighter voice at the next paragraph instead of stopping, and switches back when it can. Tap the voice name or the ⓘ next to it in the player to see why. With Pro, you can avoid it by preparing the audio ahead: in a document, tap ··· › Prepare Audio.

How do I save battery?

Prepared audio plays back on far less battery than making speech live. With Pro, you can prepare a document ahead, or let your iPhone prepare it while it charges overnight (Prepare While Charging in Settings). Anyone can turn on Battery Saver in Settings to use a Lite voice when the iPhone is locked and not charging.

A word is pronounced wrong.

Press and hold the word while reading, then choose Fix Pronunciation. Your fix is used every time the word comes up.

Larksong reads page headers, page numbers or references.

Larksong skips headers, footers, page numbers and footnotes it recognizes. Skipped text stays on screen in grey. To change what's skipped, open ··· › Reading Rules in a document, or Settings › Reading Rules for all documents.

It's using a lot of storage.

Prepared audio is kept so it can play again instantly. Open Settings › Audio Storage to see how much it uses, set a limit, or clear it. Larksong removes the audio you listened to longest ago first.

Does Larksong upload my documents?

No. Documents and audio stay on your iPhone. Larksong goes online only to load a link you add, to let iOS download the Lite voices from Apple, and for purchases. See the Privacy Policy.
