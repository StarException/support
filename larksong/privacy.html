Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Larksong does not collect any of your personal information. There is no account, no server of ours, and no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools. The voices run on your iPhone, so the text you listen to is never uploaded.

What stays on your iPhone

The following is stored only on your iPhone. We cannot see it and it is never sent to us:

  • Your documents: copies of the files you add, pages you save from a link or from Safari, pages you scan (saved as a PDF), and text you paste.
  • Your library and progress: titles, where you stopped in each document, reading rules and pronunciation fixes.
  • Prepared audio: the speech Larksong makes from your documents, kept so it can play again without being made twice. You can see and clear it in Settings › Audio Storage.
  • Free-trial usage: how much of the free listening time for the natural voices you have used. It is kept in this iPhone's Keychain so it is not reset when you reinstall the app.
  • Settings: voice, speed and other choices.

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, your documents, library and settings are included in that backup, which Apple or your computer handles. Prepared audio, voice models and the diagnostics log are left out of backups.

When Larksong goes online

Larksong works offline. It connects to the internet only in these cases, each started by you or by iOS:

  • Adding a web link: when you paste a link, Larksong downloads that one page or file directly from the website, the same way a browser would, without cookies or a login. When you share a page from Safari, Safari hands the page to Larksong on your iPhone; if the page is a PDF or other file, Larksong downloads that file from the website. Nothing passes through servers of ours.
  • Lite voices: after you install Larksong, iOS downloads the Lite voices (about 160 MB) from Apple's servers. This only fetches the voice files.
  • Purchases: buying or restoring Natural Voices goes through Apple's App Store, which also tells Larksong whether you own it. We never receive your payment information.

Apple voices are part of iOS. If you download better Apple voices in the Settings app, iOS downloads them, not Larksong.

Permissions

  • Camera: asked the first time you scan printed pages. The photos are read on your iPhone and saved in your library as a PDF; they are not uploaded.

Larksong does not use the microphone, your location, contacts or photo library.

Diagnostics

Larksong keeps a technical log on your iPhone: voice engine events, how fast speech was made, the numbers of sentences that failed, interruptions such as phone calls, page and character counts when importing, and purchase events. It does not include the text or titles of your documents. The log leaves your iPhone only if you tap Settings › Send Diagnostics and choose where to send it, for example an email to us.

Children

We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
